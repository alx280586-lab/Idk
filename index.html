Experimental AI Model

This experimental visualization blends a simulated AI forecast model with stochastic weather drivers (CAPE, dewpoint, shear) to produce evolving tornado probabilities across the continental United States.

Every update seeds new virtual storm environments, applies Gaussian smoothing to emulate synoptic-scale signals, and classifies risk levels according to SPC-like thresholds.
